Entity Relationships — Complete Reference

# Entity A Cardinality Entity B FK / Join Business Rule
1 ROLE 1 → MANY USER USER.role_id → ROLE.role_id Each user is assigned exactly one role; one role can be assigned to many users
2 ROLE M ↔ N PERMISSION ROLE_PERMISSION (role_id, permission_id) A role holds many permissions; a permission can belong to many roles
3 USER 1 → 0..1 TECHNICIAN TECHNICIAN.user_id → USER.user_id A technician IS-A user (specialisation); a user may optionally be a technician
4 USER 1 → MANY WAREHOUSE WAREHOUSE.manager_user_id → USER.user_id A manager (user) can manage multiple warehouses
5 CUSTOMER 1 → MANY ORDER ORDER.customer_id → CUSTOMER.customer_id A customer can place many orders; each order belongs to one customer
6 USER 1 → MANY ORDER ORDER.created_by_user_id → USER.user_id Orders may be entered by an employee or manager on a customer's behalf
7 ORDER 1 → MANY ORDER_ITEM ORDER_ITEM.order_id → ORDER.order_id Each order has one or more line items (products ordered)
8 PRODUCT 1 → MANY ORDER_ITEM ORDER_ITEM.product_id → PRODUCT.product_id A product can appear in many order items; each line item references one product
9 CATEGORY 1 → MANY PRODUCT PRODUCT.category_id → CATEGORY.category_id Each product belongs to one category; a category can group many products
10 SUPPLIER 1 → MANY PRODUCT PRODUCT.supplier_id → SUPPLIER.supplier_id A supplier can supply many products; each product has one primary supplier
11 PRODUCT 1 → MANY INVENTORY INVENTORY.product_id → PRODUCT.product_id A product can have inventory records in multiple warehouses
12 WAREHOUSE 1 → MANY INVENTORY INVENTORY.warehouse_id → WAREHOUSE.warehouse_id A warehouse stores many products; each inventory record is for one warehouse
13 ORDER 1 → 1 INVOICE INVOICE.order_id → ORDER.order_id (UNIQUE) Each placed order automatically generates exactly one invoice
14 CUSTOMER 1 → MANY INVOICE INVOICE.customer_id → CUSTOMER.customer_id A customer accumulates many invoices (one per order) over their lifetime
15 INVOICE 1 → MANY PAYMENT PAYMENT.invoice_id → INVOICE.invoice_id An invoice can be paid in multiple transactions (partial payments, retries, refunds)
16 CUSTOMER 1 → MANY PAYMENT PAYMENT.customer_id → CUSTOMER.customer_id Allows full payment history retrieval per customer independently of invoice lookup
17 PAYMENT_METHOD 1 → MANY PAYMENT PAYMENT.payment_method_id → PAYMENT_METHOD.payment_method_id Tracks which payment method was used per transaction; supports multiple methods
18 USER 1 → MANY CAMPAIGN CAMPAIGN.created_by_user_id → USER.user_id A user (marketing manager) can create and manage many campaigns
19 CAMPAIGN 1 → MANY CAMPAIGN_PERFORMANCE CAMPAIGN_PERFORMANCE.campaign_id → CAMPAIGN.campaign_id Performance metrics are captured as time-series snapshots per campaign day
20 ORDER 1 → MANY INSTALLATION INSTALLATION.order_id → ORDER.order_id An order for smart home products may require one or more installation jobs
21 CUSTOMER 1 → MANY INSTALLATION INSTALLATION.customer_id → CUSTOMER.customer_id A customer can have multiple installations at different times or addresses
22 TECHNICIAN 1 → MANY INSTALLATION INSTALLATION.technician_id → TECHNICIAN.technician_id A technician is assigned to many installations; each job has one assigned technician
23 USER 1 → MANY INSTALLATION INSTALLATION.scheduled_by_user_id → USER.user_id An admin or employee schedules installations on behalf of customers
24 USER 1 → MANY INVOICE INVOICE.deleted_by_user_id → USER.user_id Invoice soft-delete is admin-only; the deleting admin is audited
